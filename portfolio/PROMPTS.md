## User-directed concurrency change during development panel

**Reported:** The user requested multiple simultaneous container instances, then an aggressive allocation leaving resources for macOS and personal use. **Verified:** Host inspection found 14 logical CPUs and 24 GiB RAM. After the original 16 attempts ended naturally, the shared Docker VM increased from 2 CPUs/3 GiB to 12 CPUs/16 GiB and the coordinator resumed with 14 workers. Existing per-container limits, agent prompts, model settings, task bytes, trial counts and budget controls remained unchanged. No trial was repeated for this change. **Inferred:** Most observed waiting was on remote inference; concurrency can overlap that waiting, but does not guarantee a fixed speedup. Details are in `evidence/parallelism-amendment.json`.
